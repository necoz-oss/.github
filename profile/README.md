# Necoz

**Small. Simple. Useful.** 😸

Welcome to **Necoz**, a friendly open-source community founded by [nyaomaru](https://github.com/nyaomaru)!

We're here to build and grow small, simple, and useful open-source projects together.

We believe that even the smallest contribution can make something better.

Nyaomaru founded Necoz. The community grows when people participate, learn, and contribute together.

Our mission is to grow small open-source projects together, one contribution at a time.

## Three principles

### 🌍 Open to Everyone

Everyone is welcome, regardless of experience, technical ability, nationality, or background.

Code, documentation, bug reports, ideas, discussions, and small improvements are all valuable contributions.

### 🛠️ Build Something Useful

Projects do not need to change the world.

Solving even one person's small problem is worthwhile.

Focus on practical tools that make someone's work or life a little easier.

### 😸 Be a Good Cat

Be kind. Be curious. Respect others.

Help when possible, ask questions when needed, and provide constructive feedback.

**Good code is great. Good cats are even better!**

## Development philosophy

**Small.** Keep projects focused and avoid unnecessary complexity.

**Simple.** Make tools understandable, usable, and maintainable.

**Useful.** Solve real problems.

Great open-source software does not need to be large or complicated.

## Come and contribute

Pick a project that interests you, read its README, and start with a change that helps someone.

Look across the organization for open issues labeled [`good first issue`](https://github.com/search?q=org%3Anecoz-oss+label%3A%22good+first+issue%22+is%3Aissue+is%3Aopen&type=issues) or [`help wanted`](https://github.com/search?q=org%3Anecoz-oss+label%3A%22help+wanted%22+is%3Aissue+is%3Aopen&type=issues).

When a repository explains how to contribute, follow that. The shared guides are here:

- [Contribution guidelines](../CONTRIBUTING.md)
- [Code of Conduct](../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)

**We don't need to build something big. We just need to build something useful, together. 😸**

**Stay a Good Cat! 😸**
