# Code of Conduct

## Be a Good Cat

Necoz is a welcoming open-source community. People of different backgrounds and levels of experience should be able to participate, learn, and contribute here.

This Code of Conduct applies to Necoz organization spaces on GitHub, including repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project activity the organization maintains.

When a repository publishes its own Code of Conduct, follow that document as well.

## What we encourage

- **Kindness** — treat people with care, including people who are new or unsure
- **Curiosity** — ask questions, and stay interested in perspectives different from yours
- **Respect** — welcome different experience, nationalities, and backgrounds
- **Helpful feedback** — be specific, and aim comments at the work
- **Constructive disagreement** — challenge ideas when it improves the project, while treating people decently
- **A wide welcome** — make room for contributors whose background differs from your own

## What we prohibit

- Harassment and discrimination
- Personal attacks
- Threats and intimidation
- Deliberate exclusion of someone from the community
- Doxxing or sharing someone's private information without permission
- Repeated inappropriate behavior after a person has been asked to stop

## Reporting a concern

Questions, suggestions, and ordinary review comments belong in the project's public issues, pull requests, or discussions.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, and other inappropriate behavior need a private channel. Public GitHub Issues are the wrong place for those reports. Please keep sensitive details out of public issues, pull requests, and discussions.

Email [nyaonyao0725@gmail.com](mailto:nyaonyao0725@gmail.com) to report a Code of Conduct concern in private. Include what happened, where it happened, and who was involved, with only the details maintainers need.

Maintainers will read the report. This is not a promise of a specific response time, and it is not a promise of absolute confidentiality. Reports are shared only with people who need them to respond.

## How violations are handled

Maintainers responsible for the space will:

1. Read the report and, when they need more context, ask follow-up questions in private.
2. Keep the report as private as they practically can. Share it only with people who need it to respond.
3. Choose a response that fits what happened.
4. Tell the reporter what can be shared about the outcome, and leave out private details that are not needed.

If a report is about a maintainer, say so in the message to the address above. Necoz lists no separate contact for that case.

A different organization owner can review that report when the organization has more than one owner. With a single owner, an independent reporting or review process may not exist yet, and the person the report is about may be the only one who can read it. This document does not promise an independent review, or confidentiality beyond what that person can practically keep.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Maintainers are expected to:

- Practice this Code of Conduct in public project spaces
- Apply it consistently, including when a report involves someone they know
- Take reports of harm seriously
- Protect the reporter's identity, and the private details of a report, unless sharing them is genuinely required to handle the incident

## Possible enforcement actions

Maintainers may, depending on the situation:

- Ask for a change in behavior
- Give a warning
- Limit or pause someone's participation in organization repositories
- Remove someone from a repository or from the organization when harm is serious or repeated

Enforcement exists so people can keep building useful things together.

## Privacy

Share the information maintainers need in order to understand what happened. Leave out another person's private details beyond what the incident requires.

Maintainers should keep report information only for handling the incident, and limit who can see it. When a reporter asks to stay unnamed, maintainers should honor that whenever they can still look into the report.

**Stay a Good Cat! 😸**
